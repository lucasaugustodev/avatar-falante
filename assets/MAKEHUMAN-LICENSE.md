# MakeHuman oral assets

`avatar-falante.glb` incorporates adapted geometry from MakeHuman's
`teeth/teeth_shape01` and `tongue/tongue01`, including the tongue diffuse texture.
These assets were explicitly released under **CC0** in September 2020.

Copyright holders at release, as recorded in the source OBJ files:

- Data Collection AB
- Joel Palmius
- Jonas Hauquier

Sources:

- [Official asset catalogue](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
- [Downloaded asset pack](https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip)
- [MakeHuman asset license](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md)
- [CC0 1.0 Universal legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

Adaptations in this project: scaling and alignment to the avatar, smoothing,
custom enamel and gingiva materials, jaw weights and eight speech morph targets.
The avatar's face and other original assets are separate from these CC0 assets.
